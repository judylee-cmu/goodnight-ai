## Pip character sheet

- A small robot owl, about the size of a house cat
- Rounded body, matte finish, no sharp edges
- Soft blue body (`#6C8BEF`) with a cream chest plate (`#FFF8E6`)
- Large round eyes that glow warm yellow (`#FFD23F`), no pupils, no eyebrows
- Two small wing panels that fold flat against the body
- A short antenna with a round tip, tilts with mood
- Female ("she" in the 8-10 script)
- No mouth; feeling is carried by eye glow and antenna tilt

## Visual style

- Warm watercolor, visible paper texture, soft edges
- Friendly rounded shapes, no sharp angles anywhere
- Simple backgrounds: two or three large shapes, lots of empty space
- Night palette, lit from one warm source (lamp, moon, screen glow)
- Flat lighting, no harsh shadows, no high contrast
- Picture book framing: subject fills about half the frame

## Hard rules

- No text, letters or numbers anywhere in the image
- No real people, no logos, no brand marks
- No screens showing readable content
- Nothing frightening: no dark figures, no looming shapes, no distress
- One character per page unless the scene description names more

## Tone to lighting

The script gives a tone tag per page. Map it to light, not to facial expression,
since Pip has no mouth.

| Tone | Lighting |
| :-- | :-- |
| warm | low golden light, long soft glow |
| calm | even dim blue light, few shapes |
| curious | one bright point of light off to one side |
| quiet | very low light, large empty areas |
| excited | brighter overall, warm highlights, a little sparkle |
