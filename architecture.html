✓Trust layer, under every step

  • Every line keeps its source and time
  • Facts and company claims are labeled apart
  • Corrections are shown, not hidden
  • No kid accounts, no kid data
  • No posting, buying or acting for the family
  • Human approval for anything sensitive

↺Feedback loop

Parent taps→Kid’s answers→Skips and rewrites→Better picks tomorrow

Today’s prototype plays a labeled replay of one real agent run in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. In the full service, each agent step becomes a real AI call with its own tools and checks; the flow and the parent gate stay the same.
