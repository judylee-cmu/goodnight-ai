Why it matters

AI literacy matters, and it should start young.

Kids already use AI every day. They should also learn how it works.

2 in 3teens use AI often. Fewer than half of their parents do.
58%of parents want schools to teach responsible AI across classes.

Sources: Common Sense Media survey, March 2026 (teens 12 to 17 and their parents). Child Trends and NORC survey of 720 parents, August 2026.
